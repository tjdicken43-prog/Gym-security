How this page works

Takes about a minute each morning.

  1. We watch your turnstile at nightEvery entry is photographed and counted. People leaving are ignored.
  2. Anything odd goes to the topRed = looks like a tailgate or a shared code. Yellow = worth a quick look. Green = normal.
  3. Check the time in your scan systemEach entry shows exactly which seconds to look at. Tap what you found: that's it.

    People

    Everyone signs in with their own email. Staff check entries. Managers also run the alert list and the sneak-in price. Owners also decide who's here. Removing someone stops their sign-in straight away.

    Gym settings

    When staff or expected visitors come in without scanning, their entries can look like tailgates. List those times and flags then say "probably staff". Nothing is hidden.

    Night mode (hours nobody is at the desk): stricter alerts. Less certain flags and "maybe a second person" entries alert too, marked "worth a look". More alerts, nothing missed.
    Event mode (open house, guest night): no tailgate alerts for a few hours. Entries are still recorded.
    How long photos are kept (owner only). Shorter is more private; the monthly report then covers less.
