A note for members

To keep the gym safe and fair for everyone who pays, our entrance camera checks that each person coming in has their own scan.

What it does: it counts how many people come through the turnstile after each scan. If more people come in than scanned, our staff look at the photo.

What it doesn't do: it doesn't recognise faces, and it doesn't record video. Photos are deleted after 48 hours unless an entry needs following up.

Bringing a guest? Great: get them a day pass at the front desk first. Membership codes are for one person.

Questions? Ask at the front desk.

A line for the membership agreement

"The entrance is monitored by camera to confirm that each person entering has checked in. Photos of entries are kept for up to 48 hours (up to 35 days when needed to resolve a membership issue) and are not used for facial recognition. Membership codes and cards are for the member's use only; guests need a day pass."

Ask your lawyer to check this once for your state.

For staff: what to say

Always check the photo first. A flag means "worth a look", not "caught". Be friendly: most people simply didn't know.

Opening: "Hi! Our entrance camera shows a guest came in with you earlier without checking in. No problem: a day pass is $10. Want me to add it now?"

If they say it wasn't them: "Thanks, I'll double-check the photo." Then mark it on the gym page.

Shared code: "Membership codes are for one person. I'll need to set up a day pass for your friend."

Then tap what happened next on the gym page, so the owner sees it.
