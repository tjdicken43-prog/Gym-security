Advanced settings — most people can leave these alone
"Fast only" will miss someone walking in calmly behind another person, which is how most people actually get in at an unstaffed door. It catches running and gate-hopping. Moderate is the safer default.

Missing smaller or further-away people? Switch to "All motion" — in testing it caught children and distant figures that Moderate did not. Costs a few more analyses per night.

A spending ceiling. If something in a zone moves constantly, this stops it running up a bill. It slows down before it ever stops.
