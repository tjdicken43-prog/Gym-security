Terms of service

  • What you're buying. A hosted service that watches the entrance camera you choose during the hours you choose, and tells you (by email, phone notification, team chat or, where available, text message) when more people come through than expected. It includes your gym's activity page and a monthly summary.
  • What's at your gym. A small computer (a laptop or a Raspberry Pi) on your network next to your camera recorder. It needs power and an internet connection.
  • Billing. Charged monthly by card through Stripe until you cancel. To cancel, email or call us; it stops at the end of the month you've paid for.
  • No guarantee. SecurityAI will sometimes miss an entry or flag one that was fine, and it stops watching if the internet, power or camera goes down. It's a tool to help your staff check, not a security guarantee, and it isn't a replacement for your access control.
  • Your responsibilities. You're responsible for posting any camera signage and getting any consent your state or city requires, and for how your staff act on a flag.
  • Support. By email and phone, Monday to Friday, 9am–5pm. There's no uptime guarantee.

Privacy

  • What we collect. When something moves in the doorway during watched hours, a few photos of that moment are sent to our server and to Anthropic's Claude to count people. We also keep your contact details and the email addresses and phone numbers you add for alerts.
  • How long photos are kept. Photos of each crossing are deleted after 48 hours. Entries flagged as tailgating are kept for 35 days so they can appear in your monthly report, then deleted. No video is stored.
  • What we don't do. No facial recognition. SecurityAI does not identify anyone or match people to member records. We don't sell data or use it for advertising.
  • Who can see it. Anyone with your gym's code or a link you share, and SecurityAI for running and supporting the service. Shared photo links stop working when the photo is deleted.
  • Other things we keep. Staff logins (name, email, role), phones signed up for notifications (a push address from the browser maker, no phone number), and a nightly locked backup emailed to SecurityAI (no photos, nothing past the keep times above; SecurityAI keeps 30 days of them). Questions typed into "Ask" are answered by Anthropic's Claude using your gym's entry counts, not photos or member numbers.
  • When the site fixes itself. If part of the site breaks, the error text and the related code (never photos, member numbers or passwords) may be sent to Anthropic's Claude to suggest a fix. A person at SecurityAI approves every code change.
  • Member numbers. Scrambled on the gym's own computer. Only phones the owner approves can read them; our server can't.
  • Team chat alerts. If the gym adds a Slack, Teams, Google Chat or Discord channel, alert text (what happened, when, and a link to the gym page) is sent to that service. Photos are not.
  • On staff phones. The gym page keeps its last list on the phone for up to 48 hours so it opens without signal (times, counts and answers; no photos or member numbers). Signing out wipes it.
  • Who read member numbers. Each time an approved phone opens member numbers, the phone's name, who was signed in and the time are logged for the gym's owner (kept 90 days).
  • Shorter keep times. A gym's owner can choose to keep photos for less time than above (24 hours; 14 days for kept entries). Never longer.
  • Payments. Card details are entered on Stripe's page and never reach our server.
